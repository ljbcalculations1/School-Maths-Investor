About this calculator: The "Indexation" Trap

When Capital Gains Tax (CGT) is charged on investments without adjusting for inflation (indexation allowance), you can be taxed on "paper gains" that represent no real increase in purchasing power.

This calculator illustrates how tax on inflationary growth can erode your real wealth over time.

The Capital Gains Allowance is included so you can see how a tax-free allowance reduces the amount of gain subject to CGT.
