Why these tools are different

Most financial calculators give you an answer. The School Maths Investor takes a different approach.

Every interactive learning tool has been created to help you explore how changing one assumption—such as time, investment return, inflation or tax—changes the outcome.

The aim isn't simply to calculate a number. The aim is to understand the mathematics behind that number.

Because when you understand why investing works, you are much better placed to judge when it works.

About The School Maths Investor

The School Maths Investor was created to help bridge the financial advice gap by connecting school mathematics with real financial decisions.

Whether you're learning about investing for the first time, improving your financial literacy or simply curious about how mathematics explains the world around us, these interactive calculators are designed to help you build understanding rather than memorise rules.
