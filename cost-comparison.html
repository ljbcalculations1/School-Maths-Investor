About this calculator

Investment costs can dramatically affect your long-term wealth.

Percentage-based fees may start small, but the amount you pay increases as your portfolio grows. Over long periods, the effect can be amplified because money paid in fees is no longer available to compound.

Use this calculator to compare two fee structures side-by-side over time to find the most cost-effective option for your strategy.

Important information

This calculator is provided for educational and illustrative purposes only. It is designed to demonstrate the mathematical effect of investment fees and is not financial advice or a recommendation to use or avoid any particular provider or product.

The calculations use the assumptions entered by the user and simplify real-world investment costs. Actual charges, investment returns and other costs may differ. The calculator does not take account of every possible charge, tax, investment cost, contribution, withdrawal or change in fee structure.

The results are projections based on the assumptions entered and are not predictions of future investment performance. You should consider your own circumstances and, where appropriate, seek professional financial advice before making investment decisions.
