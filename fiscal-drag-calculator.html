About this calculator: The "Fiscal Drag" Trap

When inflation pushes up wages and the cost of living, but the government freezes tax bands, you get pushed into higher tax brackets without actually getting richer. This is known as "Fiscal Drag".

This calculator shows how keeping tax thresholds static during inflation results in a massive, hidden cut to your real living standards.
