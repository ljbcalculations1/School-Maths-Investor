About the Data

This calculator uses historical returns from a globally diversified all-cap equity portfolio.

The returns include reinvested dividends and have been adjusted for inflation, meaning all results are shown in today's money.

The purpose is not to predict future returns, but to demonstrate how regular investing has worked through different market environments, including periods of strong growth and significant market falls.

Past performance does not guarantee future results.
