About School Maths Investor

I'm Lee Bailey, a former maths teacher with 26 years' classroom experience and over 30 years as a private investor.

My aim is to show that the mathematics we learn at school forms the foundation of financial literacy.

Every calculator on this site is designed not just to calculate an answer, but to explain the mathematics behind it.
